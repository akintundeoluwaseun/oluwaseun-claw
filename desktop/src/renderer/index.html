                          Browser

                          A metasearch server you or your organisation runs. Claw only talks to it over the network; it is not part of Claw, and it needs its own internet connection.

                          Claw uses its own browser profile. It never sees your passwords or signed-in accounts.

                          Limits

                          Jobs that run on a schedule

                          They run only while S-CLAW is open. A computer that is off, asleep or logged out does nothing, whatever the schedule says. And because nobody is watching, a scheduled job can only look — anything that would normally ask your permission is refused, and the job stops and tells you.

                            What Claw remembers

                            Only what you have told it: how you like things done, what you are working on, the people you deal with, and what was decided. It never keeps a password, a code, a card number or a key — those are refused when you say them, not hidden afterwards.
