---
name: make-a-promo-video
description: Make a short animated promo video (motion graphics) for a product, business or event, in After Effects or Claw's built-in maker.
triggers: video, motion graphics, animation, animated, after effects, mp4
---
1. Call make_motion_video once with:
   - title: the product or business name, short.
   - subtitle: one line saying what it is.
   - points: three or four short selling points, each a few words.
   - contact: a phone, WhatsApp or website line only if the person gave one.
   - seconds: 10 unless the person asked for another length.
   - size: "story" for WhatsApp status or reels, "square" for Instagram posts, else "landscape".
   - style: "pop" for lively, "fade" for calm, else "slide".
   - background and accent: colours as codes like "#14213d" and "#f2a541".
2. Do not open After Effects yourself or click through it: make_motion_video drives it when it is installed.
3. Report where the video was saved and which maker made it. Do not describe a video you did not make.
