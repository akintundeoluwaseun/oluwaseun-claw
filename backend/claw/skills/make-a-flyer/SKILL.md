---
name: make-a-flyer
description: Make a promotional flyer, poster or advert for a product, business or event and save it.
triggers: flyer, poster, advert, promo, promotional, banner
---
1. Call make_flyer once with:
   - title: the product or business name, short.
   - subtitle: one line saying what it is.
   - points: three or four short selling points.
   - contact: a phone, WhatsApp or website line only if the person gave one.
   - background and accent: colours as codes like "#14213d" and "#f2a541".
   - images: leave this out unless the person named a picture file.
2. Report where the flyer was saved. Do not describe a flyer you did not make.
